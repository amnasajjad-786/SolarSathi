# User testing script (≈10 min per person, need ≥ 3)

Find people who **have solar + battery at home** (family, neighbours, colleagues, a local solar installer).
Open the deployed link on *their* phone.

## Before showing the app (2 min)
1. What happens at your house during load-shedding at night?
2. Has your battery ever died before the light came back? What did you do?
3. How do you decide what to switch off? Do you check your inverter app?

## Tasks (5 min) — don't help unless they're stuck
1. "Tell the app your battery % — try using a screenshot of your inverter app."
2. "Add your area's load-shedding times." (let them paste their real SMS/WhatsApp)
3. "Will your battery last tonight? What does the app want you to do?"
4. "Turn off the AC in the list — what changed?"
5. Switch to Roman Urdu — "Is this easier to read?"

## After (3 min)
- Would you actually do what the plan says? Which step would you skip?
- Was any number wrong for your house (battery size, appliance watts)?
- Would you open this every evening? Would you pay Rs 200/month? Would you want it on WhatsApp instead?
